THE CHALLENGE

Methane is the second most important gas causing global warming.

Because methane lifetime is about a decade, many scientists and policymakers prioritize its mitigation. While mitigating anthropogenic sources of atmospheric methane is important, wetlands are the single largest global source of atmospheric methane emissions. (fn1) Moreover, warming-induced feedback could significantly amplify future warming — by as much as 0.4°C by 2100 — which "most climate models do not yet represent". (fn2)

Warming → more wetland methane → greater radiative forcing → more warming

WHAT WE DO

Accelerate methane oxidation. Without producing ozone. Without lowering water levels.

Nature already oxidizes methane in the atmosphere. But it takes about a decade to remove methane. We develop technology to accelerate such removal by orders of magnitude. Without increasing ozone above ambient levels. Without decreasing water table. Verifiable and additional removal.
